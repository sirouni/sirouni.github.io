Privacy Policy

Stacked Arrows · Last updated: October 19, 2026

Summary

Stacked Arrows does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools in the app.

Data stored on your device

Your best results, settings, and the days you have already cleared are stored on your device (in app storage and the keychain). This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Game Center

The Daily leaderboard uses Apple Game Center. Your score, and the display name and rank Game Center shows, are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive any profile data.

Checking today's date

To keep the Daily cave fair, the app asks a few public web servers (such as apple.com, cloudflare.com, google.com and microsoft.com) for the current time. Those requests contain no information about you beyond what any network request includes, such as your IP address, which those servers' operators may see under their own policies. We do not run these servers and do not receive that data.

Photos

If you choose to save a score card, the app asks for permission to add the image to your photo library. It can only add images and cannot read your photos.

Notifications

If you allow them, the app schedules local reminders on your device about the daily cave. No data leaves your device for this, and you can turn them off in Settings at any time.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
