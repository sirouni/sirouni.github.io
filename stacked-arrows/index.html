Stacked Arrows

One button. One cave a day.

Hold to climb, release to fall, and a quick tap slows the dive. Every arrow blasts a hole where it ends, so the next arrow can fly deeper. Reach the goal with as few arrows as you can.

Daily cave

A new cave every day (UTC), the same for everyone. Fewest arrows wins, and ties go to the fewer taps. Rank on the Game Center leaderboard.

Endless

One cave that never ends. Pass checkpoints to earn more arrows and see how far you get.

Free

Pick any cave and practise as much as you like. It never touches the leaderboard.

No ads. No in-app purchases.
